What every winery already knows

The tools that built the industry can't scale with it.

Three structural failures define every incumbent platform — and together, they erode the member relationships that took years to build.

🧩
Fragmented operations
Shipments are in one system, member email in another, compliance in a third. Staff spend more time reconciling data between platforms than serving members. Errors compound at scale.
Avg. 3.2 disconnected tools per winery
🔭
Zero predictive visibility
Current platforms report on what already happened. By the time you see a cancellation, the member has been disengaging for 90 days. Reactive outreach converts at roughly one-third the rate of predictive intervention.
67% of churned members showed signals 60d prior
📱
Member portals from 2012
Self-service portals across the category are visually dated, require password resets to access, and offer no personalization. Members who find the portal difficult to use skip shipments at 2.4× the baseline rate.
43% of club members never log in to their portal
Full platform coverage

Every surface of your club, in one place.

Vinifera covers the complete operational arc — from the moment a member signs up to the final bottle in their cellar — with purpose-built tools that remove friction at every handoff.

Operations Dashboard
A live command center for every active release: pipeline status, billing capture rate, shipment progress, and AI-surfaced at-risk members — visible the moment you open the app.
Member CRM
Full-profile member records: purchase and visit history, AI inferred taste profiles, engagement scores, and a built-in task and notes system for every staff interaction.
Shipment Management
Bulk label generation across UPS, FedEx, and regional carriers. Address validation, signature requirements, and failed-delivery resolution integrated into a single filterable table.
Analytics & Cohort Reporting
Cohort retention heatmaps, acquisition channel LTV analysis, revenue-by-tier decomposition, and churn driver attribution — the data that informs pricing and benefit decisions.
Club Tier Builder
Design tiered membership programs with configurable benefits, bottle counts, skip/swap rules, and pricing. Adjust on the fly without contacting a support team.
Vintage Allocations
Reserve limited-production wines exclusively for club members before they reach retail. Manage claim windows, auto-remind unclaimed members, and release remnants on a schedule.
Release Schedule
Full-year calendar view of every release, billing date, notification window, and member customization deadline. Milestone tracking ties each step to accountable staff action.
Fulfillment & Compliance
Automatic state-by-state DTC permit checking, real-time carrier routing, and TTB report preparation. Ship to 38 states without a compliance specialist on staff.
Communications Suite
AI-written, segment-specific emails generated in seconds. Template library, approval workflows, A/B testing, and full open/click analytics — native to the platform, not bolted on.
Loyalty & Rewards
Three-tier loyalty program with configurable earn rules, a redeemable rewards catalog, and a skip-penalty option that measurably reduces consecutive shipment abandonment.
Integrations & API
Native connections to Stripe, Klaviyo, ShipCompliant, UPS, and FedEx. A full REST API and webhook system let you connect your POS, tasting room software, or BI tools.
Personalized Member Portal
A mobile-first self-service portal with magic-link login, AI wine recommendations, bottle swap and skip controls, loyalty points tracking, and membership management — all without a phone call.
Built-in intelligence

The industry's first wine club platform where AI earns its place.

Vinifera's AI doesn't live in a separate tab or require a data science team. It runs continuously in the background, surfacing the interventions that preserve revenue and the recommendations that deepen loyalty.

Churn Prediction Engine
43 behavioral signals — email opens, shipment skips, tasting room visits, portal logins, purchase frequency — combined into a daily risk score for every member. Intervention at 70%+ risk has measured a 71% reduction in actual cancellations.
AI Taste Profile Inference
Purchase patterns, add-on selections, and swap history train a per-member preference model. The output drives personalized email copy, portal wine recommendations, and curated allocation offers — without a single survey.
AI Email Copywriter
Generate segment-specific, brand-voice-matched emails for any release event in under 30 seconds. Personalization tokens, winemaker note integration, and tone controls are built in. Average open rate across AI-generated campaigns: 52%.
Upgrade & Revenue Opportunity Alerts

How a release works

From wine selection to front door in four steps.

Vinifera structures the release workflow so nothing falls through — each step gates the next, and every stakeholder knows exactly what requires their attention.

1
Configure the Release
Select wines, reserve inventory, set the allocation window, and define per-tier bottle counts and pricing — in one screen.
2
Notify Members
AI-drafted, personalized emails go to each tier segment. Members customize bottles, addresses, and quantities through the portal before the cutoff date.
3
Capture Billing
One-click billing run charges all active members. Failed payments enter an automatic retry sequence. Dunning emails reduce write-offs without staff intervention.
4
Ship & Track
Multi-carrier labels generate in bulk. Compliance is verified per shipment. Members receive branded tracking emails automatically. Exceptions surface in a dedicated queue.
